Use case: motif sheet for a seamless printed scarf pattern (the motifs will be cut out and re-arranged by software).
Subject: small olive branches, each with 5-9 slim leaves and 2-4 olives, in a few different shapes and directions (straight, gently curved, pointing left/right/diagonal). About 12-16 whole branches, plus a few loose single olives and single leaves as small fillers.
Background: one flat solid cream #F4EBDD filling the whole canvas, no texture, no paper grain, no vignette.
Palette: olive green #7A8B4F leaves, dark olive #4B5A2E stems and leaf veins, olive purple #6B4E71 olives (some olives dark olive green #4B5A2E for variety).
Style: refined minimal flat illustration like a quality fashion print, crisp clean edges, no outlines in other colours, no shadows, no gradients.
Layout: motifs evenly spaced with clear cream space between them; EVERY motif fully inside the canvas, none touching or cut by the edges, none overlapping each other. No text.
