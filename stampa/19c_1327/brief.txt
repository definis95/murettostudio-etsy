Use case: motif sheet for a seamless printed scarf pattern (the motifs will be cut out and re-arranged by software).
Subject: tiny tossed wildflower bouquets with thin stems: chamomile daisies, red poppies, lavender sprigs, small mustard buttercups. About 12-16 whole little bouquets of different mixes and directions, plus a few loose single small flowers and tiny leaves as fillers.
Background: one flat solid warm off-white #F7F1E6 filling the whole canvas, no texture, no paper grain, no vignette.
Palette: terracotta #C9704F poppies, sage #8FAE8B stems and leaves, lavender #A897C9, mustard #D9A441 centres and buttercups, white petals with a thin sage edge for chamomile.
Style: refined minimal flat illustration like a quality fashion print, crisp clean edges, no shadows, no gradients. Stems not thinner than a fine pen line.
Layout: motifs evenly spaced with clear background space between them; EVERY motif fully inside the canvas, none touching or cut by the edges, none overlapping each other. No text.
