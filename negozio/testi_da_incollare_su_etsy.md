## 2. Shop Manager → Settings → Production partners

Oggi c'è un solo partner: "Stampa i tessuti", località "Europe". Va sistemato così:

Campi comuni a entrambi: "Mostra agli acquirenti" spento · Perché: "Non dispongo delle capacità tecniche o delle attrezzature" · Ruolo progettazione: "Progetto tutto personalmente" · Ruolo produttore: "Fa tutto il produttore esterno".
Dopo averli creati, elimina il vecchio "Stampa i tessuti".

**a) Europa**
- **Partner name:** `Printify print partners Europe`
- **Titolo descrittivo** (è quello che vedono i clienti): `Print-on-demand partner in Europe`
- **Location:** `Germany` (borse e body; le tazze sono fatte in Repubblica Ceca, ma Etsy chiede un solo paese)
- **About this partner:**
```
Print-on-demand partners in Germany and the Czech Republic. I create the designs; they print each item when it is ordered and ship it directly to the customer.
```

**b) USA**
- **Partner name:** `Printify print partners USA`
- **Titolo descrittivo:** `Print-on-demand partner in the USA`
- **Location:** `United States`
- **What they do:** "They print and ship my designs"
- **About this partner:**
```
Print-on-demand partners in the USA for some of our kids' tees. I create the designs; they print each item when it is ordered and ship it directly to the customer.
```

Quando pubblicheremo le inserzioni, ogni prodotto verrà collegato al partner giusto: Europa per borse, tazze e body, USA per le t-shirt bambino a maniche lunghe.

## 3. FAQ (Shop → Edit shop → FAQs)

Cancella le vecchie FAQ e inserisci queste.

**Q:** Where are your products made?
**A:**
```
All ideas are created in Italy. Each item is printed on demand by our production partners: European products are made in Germany or the Czech Republic, and some kids' tees are made in the USA.
```

**Q:** Which countries do you ship to?
**A:**
```
To keep delivery fast and avoid customs fees, items made in Europe ship to Europe and the UK, and items made in the USA ship to the USA and Canada. Each listing shows where it can be delivered.
```

**Q:** How long will my order take?
**A:**
```
Production takes 2-5 business days, then delivery time depends on your country and is shown at checkout. You will receive a tracking number by email.
```

**Q:** What are the baby and kids' clothes made of?
**A:**
```
All baby and kids' garments are 100% cotton; our baby bodysuits are certified organic cotton. Please check the size chart in each listing before ordering.
```

**Q:** Are your designs made with AI?
**A:**
```
Every design starts from my own idea and brief. The artwork is created with the help of AI image tools, then checked and prepared for printing by me.
```

**Q:** My item arrived damaged. What should I do?
**A:**
```
Send me a message with a photo within 14 days of delivery and I will send a free replacement or a full refund.
```
